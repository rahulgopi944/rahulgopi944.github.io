About us

A small team for your people and your systems

RasNode is a recruitment and IT consulting firm in Kochi, hiring across industries, with one point of contact for your people and your systems.

Built by people who know hiring and IT

RasNode was founded by Amir, Shalumon and Rahul, who have spent the past five years in recruitment and IT consulting. We act as the node between your business, the talent it needs and the systems it runs on, with one point of contact for all three.

We've seen how hiring and software problems feed each other. A great team slows down on the wrong system, and a great system fails without the right people. So we handle both, with the care of a small team that knows your business by name.
