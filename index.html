    Any questions?

    Quick answers about how we work. Can't find yours? Ask us.

    What kinds of roles do you recruit for?

    IT and non-IT roles across industries: developers, QA and IT support, as well as sales, accounts and finance, HR, operations, retail, healthcare, manufacturing, logistics and hospitality teams.

    Is it permanent hiring?

    Mostly, yes. Our core is permanent placements, and we shape every search around what your company needs, from one key hire to a growing team.

    What does your IT consulting cover?

    We look at how your business runs, recommend the systems that fit, then set them up, tailor them and train your team, with support after go-live.

    I’m looking for a job. How do I apply?

    Send us your CV through the contact page. We’ll get in touch when a role matches your skills and what you’re looking for.

    Will my details be kept confidential?

    Yes. Client openings, candidate profiles and business details are kept private and shared only when needed for the role.
